Hi, my name is **Steven Reitsma**.
Years ago I started out as a data scientist but soon found out that my real passion is in data engineering and platform infrastructure.
Now, I have my own freelancing company and lead a team at the National Cyber Security Center.
Here, I build a data platform that is used by cybersecurity experts to create products that protect critical organisations in the Netherlands.

I'm a firm believer of open source technology and have committed to many [open source projects](https://github.com/StevenReitsma), such as ClickHouse, MinIO, Airflow, Kubernetes and more.

In my free time I like to travel and shoot video along the way.
I've also directed my own game-show, which I edited into a [feature film](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt22092762/).
I also like to work on my homelab, brew my own beer and regularly go to music festivals.
